# Krosforge 0.0.10

- Earn five characteristic points and five maximum life per level gained. The **Characteristics** panel lets you allocate, redistribute, and reset points for all six primary attributes.
- Restore spent and available points after reconnecting. Existing characters can spend the points earned from their saved level.
- Refresh the open **Characteristics** panel after allocating points so its life total stays in sync with the HUD.
- Show the native level-up animation and dialog when you return from a fight that raises your level. Automatic feature announcements no longer cover the dialog in local sessions.
- Open the **Spells** window to inspect class and common spells. Available grades update with the character's level.
- Drag unlocked spells onto the spell bar and rearrange or remove shortcuts. Each character's layout is restored after reconnecting.
- Keep the Enutrof air attack that grants Power in its assigned slot when entering battle. Supported combat spells now use their highest unlocked grade.

## Compatibility

The supported client build is **3.7.14.18**. The spell window displays catalog data. Combat uses the saved slots for spells supported by the local combat engine.

The local database migrates to schema 6 with a verified backup before adding characteristic allocations and spell shortcuts. The launcher updates the prepared client copy in place. To downgrade, use a compatible executable and restore its matching database backup in a separate portable data directory.
