# Krosforge 0.0.7

- Encounter a monster group on the starting map, approach it and enter combat.
- Choose a starting position, confirm readiness and take turns using action points and movement points.
- Move around occupied cells and terrain obstacles, and cast supported spells with range, line-of-sight and cast-limit checks.
- Apply elemental damage, resistance, weaknesses, critical hits and defeat. The opponent approaches, attacks and ends its turn automatically.
- Display victory, defeat and abandonment results, including effective damage dealt, damage received and enemies defeated.
- Return to the exploration map with full life, action points and movement points while preserving the confirmed exploration position.
- Award one unit of local currency per victory. Save the reward and outcome together so reconnecting or repeating a confirmation cannot duplicate the payment.
- Recover interrupted combat after reconnecting or restarting without awarding an unfinished fight.
- Initialize the monster tooltip and show the ready button text on the first encounter.

## Validation and limitations

Combat was validated on Windows with one local account and the Portuguese interface, including preparation, movement, two class spells, the opponent's attacks, victory, natural defeat, abandonment and restart recovery. Automated coverage includes TCP disconnection before the final animation acknowledgement, concurrent duplicate payouts, transaction rollback and persistent balances. Combat with multiple accounts and the other interface languages has not yet been validated.

This ruleset contains one configured encounter, a shared unarmed action and two level-one ranged actions for one class. Other classes currently expose only the shared action. Equipment effects, buffs, summons, tackle and erosion are not implemented. Victories award one unit of local currency under an explicit local rule; experience, level progression and item loot are not awarded. The detailed participant statistics tab is not populated.

Windows x64 packages contain one portable `Krosforge.exe` inside a ZIP. Reference client build: `3.7.14.18`. Monster appearance, attributes, spells and map geometry are read from the user's separately installed client; no game assets are bundled.

Existing profiles, settings, characters and the shared client copy are preserved. The database is migrated automatically to schema 3 to add combat outcomes and character wallets, with a backup taken before migration. An unfinished fight is closed on reconnect; its exploration position is retained. Completed rewards persist, but result screens are not replayed after reconnecting.
